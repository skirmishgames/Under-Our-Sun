Missions to print

Survivor log

A save card for roguelike play. One card tracks one character between missions: health, water, points, conditions, dice upgrades, items and the missions played. Cards are 135 x 100 mm, four to a page. Print at 100% and cut along the marks, or keep the page whole as a group sheet.

Guide

How a mission is built

A mission is a short deck of numbered parts. Players read part 1 at setup and each part tells them when to read the next one. The editor starts every new mission from the community mission template: briefing, setup, special rules, mission goal and objectives on part 1, story on the middle parts, success and failure on the last part.

Empty blocks are left off the card, so you can keep the template blocks you do not need without them printing.

Block types

  • Section with heading: a panel with a heading such as BRIEFING, SETUP or SPECIAL RULES.
  • Story text: narrative with no heading.
  • Mission goal, Objectives, Bonus objective, Success, Failure: centred text under a coloured label.
  • Picture: a map setup diagram or similar, with an optional caption.

Item names

The "Insert item" list in each block holds every craftable item and piece of gear from the crafting overview, grouped as tools, gear, healing and weapons. It adds the name in bold, for example Binoculars, so setup and reward text matches the cards in the box. Items marked CP01 need that set.

Icons

Text that does not fit

Text sits at the bottom of the card and the art shows above it. When a part gets long, the font shrinks a little to fit. If it still does not fit, the editor marks the part in red. Split it into another part or trim it.

Sharing missions

Export a mission from the editor to get a .json file with its text and pictures. Anyone can import that file on the Missions page. Use "Back up all" now and then, because clearing browser data deletes saved missions.
